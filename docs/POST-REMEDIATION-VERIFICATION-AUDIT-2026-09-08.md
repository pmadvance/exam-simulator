## Is the dashboard now meaningful?

Yes, provided the content prerequisites are completed. The dashboard now answers four questions a serious learner needs:

1. **Am I improving on unfamiliar material?** First-seen and smoothed first-seen accuracy exclude gains caused only by repeating remembered questions.
2. **Am I prepared across the real blueprint?** ECO/domain mastery is weighted against the applicable PMP or CAPM outline and shows sample size and evidence confidence.
3. **Can I sustain exam conditions?** Comparable full simulations feed consistency, unanswered rate, pacing, and opening/middle/final-third stamina.
4. **What should I do next?** Recommendations identify weak domains, recurring mistakes, insufficient fresh coverage, pacing problems, excessive repeats, or the need for another distinct simulation.

The readiness indicator is intentionally gated. It requires two different full simulations, broad fresh-question coverage, sufficient blueprint coverage, and at least 90% content-classification coverage. It is labelled as a coaching indicator, not an official PMI score or pass guarantee.

### Why both taxonomy views matter

- **ECO domain** identifies the competency or outcome being tested. It helps a learner see whether People, Process, Business Environment, or a CAPM blueprint area is weak.
- **Delivery Approach** identifies the operating context: Predictive, Adaptive/Agile, Hybrid, or approach-agnostic. It helps expose a learner who performs well in one style but misapplies judgment in another.

These views are complementary. One tells the learner *what capability* needs work; the other tells them *in which project context* their decisions break down.

## Recommended acceptance steps before demonstrating to a client

1. Editorially classify the remaining 25 questions by Delivery Approach.
2. Add at least two distinct, blueprint-balanced full simulations to the product being demonstrated.
3. Create a dedicated production QA learner, grant a controlled test enrollment, and generate several planned attempt profiles: weak/strong, slow/fast, repeat-heavy, and end-of-test fatigue.
4. Confirm the dashboard recommendations for each profile with a qualified PMP/CAPM content reviewer.
5. Re-run the authenticated journey and, when payment testing resumes, ToyyibPay sandbox/live acceptance separately.
